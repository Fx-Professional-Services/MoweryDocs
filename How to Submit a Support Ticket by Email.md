You can create a support ticket in Redmine by sending an email to the support address.

## 1. Create a New Email

Open your email and create a new message.

Use the following format:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|Email Field|What to Enter|
|To|tickets@fxprofessionalservices.com|
|Subject|Issue title|
|Email body|Description of the issue and any relevant details|
|Attachment|Attach any relevant files or screenshots|

### Example

Subject: Unable to Open Customer Invoice

Email body:

I am unable to open the customer invoice. The page shows an error when I click the invoice.

Attachment: Screenshot of the error message

## 2. Send the Email

Send the email to:

tickets@fxprofessionalservices.com

Redmine will automatically create a ticket from your email.

## 3. Check Your Confirmation Email

You will receive a confirmation email from Redmine.

The email will include:

- The ticket number, shown in Redmine as Feature #
    
- A link to the ticket
    

Click the blue ticket link in the email.

## 4. Set Up Your Redmine Password

The ticket link will open the Redmine login page.

1. Click Lost password.
    
2. Enter the same email address you used to report the issue.
    
3. Check your email for the password reset message.
    
4. Click the password reset link.
    
5. Follow the instructions to create your password.
    

## 5. Sign In to Redmine

Return to the Redmine login page.

Enter:

- Login: The email address you used to report the issue
    
- Password: Your newly created password
    

Click Log in.

## 6. View Your Support Tickets

After you sign in, you will be directed to the Redmine Support Inbox project.

You can view the issues you have reported as tickets.

Each ticket contains the information submitted in your email, including the issue title, description, and attachments.

### Email-to-Ticket Reference

When submitting an issue by email, use:

- Subject → Issue title
    
- Email body → Issue description
    
- Attachment → Ticket attachment
    

This allows Redmine to create your support ticket with the information you provided.
