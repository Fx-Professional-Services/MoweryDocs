## View the Monthly Tune-Up Schedule

Use the Monthly Tune-Up Schedule to view the current and upcoming tune-up dates.

## Steps

1. From the **Service Management System** screen, locate **Monthly Tune-Up Schedule**.
    
2. Select **Monthly Tune-Up Schedule**.
    

The schedule displays the current date followed by upcoming tune-up dates.

## How the Schedule Works

- The **current date** is always the first date in the list.
    
- **Past dates** are automatically removed from the schedule.
    
- **Upcoming dates** remain visible below the current date.
    
- The schedule does not display dates from previous days.
