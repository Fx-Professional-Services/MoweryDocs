
## 1. View Your Assigned Tickets

1. Click My Page.
    
2. Find Issues assigned to me.
    
3. Open a ticket to view its details.
    

---

## 2. Create a Ticket

1. Open the project.
    
2. Click Issues.
    
3. Click New issue.
    
4. Select the Tracker.
    
5. Enter the Subject and Description.
    
6. Set the Priority and Assignee.
    
7. Add a Parent task, if applicable.
    
8. Add a Start Date and Due Date, if necessary.
    
9. Enter the Estimated Time required to complete the task.
    
10. Click Choose Files to add attachments, if needed.
    
11. To add Watchers, select the checkboxes next to their names.
    
12. Click Create or Create and add another to create another issue.
    

---

## 3. Update a Ticket

1. Open the ticket.
    
2. Click Edit.
    
3. Update the Status.
    
4. Add your progress in Notes.
    
5. Add attachments, if needed.
    
6. Click Submit.
    

Example Note:

Completed the update and tested it on the development server.

---

## 4. Log Spent Time

1. Open the ticket.
    
2. Click Log time or Edit (pencil icon).
    
3. Enter the Spent time in the Hours field.
    
4. Select the Activity.
    
5. Add a short description in the Comment field.
    
6. Click Create.
    

Example:

- Spent time: 2h
    
- Activity: Development
    
- Comment: Fixed the customer email issue.
    

---

## 5. View Logged Time

1. Select the project from Jump to a project….
    
2. Click Spent time.
    
3. Under Filters, select the Period and User.
    
4. Click Apply.
    

---

## 6. Switch Projects

1. Click Jump to a project….
    
2. Select the project.
    

---
